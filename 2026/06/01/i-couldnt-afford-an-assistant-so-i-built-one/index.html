I Couldn’t Afford an Assistant, So I Built One

From missed calls and forgotten follow-ups to a fully automated back office — the build that runs my field-services company.

inbound calls answered
<5

30+

assistants hired
the problem wasn’t the work

Every missed call was a missed client. I’d be in the field serving papers while the phone rang. I’d finish a serve and find emails I hadn’t answered, follow-ups I’d forgotten, invoices that hadn’t gone out.

An assistant would have cost $4,000/month. I had $0 for that. So I built one instead.

before vs. after
Task Before the system Now
Inbound call at 9 PM ❌ Goes to voicemail. Client calls a competitor. ✅ Sarah answers, captures name/phone/email/case details
New client intake ❌ 20-minute email thread. Jesse types everything. ✅ Form → case created → invoice sent. Jesse’s first touch is the field.
Attempt update to client ❌ Jesse types from the field. Often delayed. ✅ Draft auto-built from the case data. Jesse reviews and sends.
Follow-up on unpaid invoice ❌ Forgotten or manually tracked in a spreadsheet. ✅ Auto-flagged in the daily brief. One click to re-send.
Proof of service form ❌ Hand-typed. 30 minutes. Prone to error. ✅ Auto-filled from Notion. Court-ready in under a minute.
End-of-day case status ❌ In Jesse’s head. Not written down. ✅ Live in Notion. Accessible from the field.
how it was built
Month 1
Phone answered, nothing tracked
Sarah AI configured. Every call logged to Notion.

Month 2
Cases tracked, invoices manual
Square integrated. Invoice auto-sent on client confirmation.

Month 3
Invoicing automated, forms manual
PDF form-fill wired. FL-330, POS-010, SC-104 generated from case data.

Month 4
End-to-end pipeline
Intake → field → proof → delivery → close. One operator, 30+ concurrent cases.
