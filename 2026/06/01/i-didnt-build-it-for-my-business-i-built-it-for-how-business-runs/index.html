I Didn’t Build It For My Business. I Built It For How Business Runs.

The system behind my field-services company is 95% industry-agnostic. Here’s why that matters.

THE UNIVERSAL LAYER

The system that runs my company has almost nothing to do with field-services work. It runs on the parts every business runs on. Capture every lead. Communicate with every customer. Get invoices out. Never forget a follow-up.

Those four things are the same whether you serve legal papers, run a shop, sell online, or build an audience.

what’s NOT in the system

There’s nothing about field-services work in the core. No legal forms as the foundation. No court scheduling as the engine. Those are the industry-specific layer on top — swappable. The base is the same for a plumber, a coach, or a real estate agent.
